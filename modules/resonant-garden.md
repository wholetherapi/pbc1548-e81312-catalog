# Resonant Garden

The garden is a bank of six tuned resonators milled from the same spruce
stock, excited by two contact piezo drivers under the front panel.  The
resonators are deliberately detuned by a few cents so slow beating appears
when two voices are close.

The r3 board replaces the hand-wired excitation bus with a buffered driver
stage and adds a voltage-controlled damping network.  Resonance tails can be
shortened without losing the low-end body of the instrument.

Front panel artwork is screen printed on brushed aluminium with a wax-filled
engraving for the tuning marks.
