# Lumen Drift

Lumen Drift is a concept for a small drone box whose pitch follows the colour
temperature of the room.  A cadmium-sulfide cell pair feeds a comparator
network that nudges two square-wave oscillators apart as the light changes.

There is no hardware beyond a paper sketch so far; the idea is on the
collective's long list.
