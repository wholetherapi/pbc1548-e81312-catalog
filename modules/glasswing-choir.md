# Glasswing Choir

Glasswing Choir is a 1U companion tile for the Resonant Garden.  The concept
uses a thin glass plate as the resonator body with two surface transducers
mounted at the antinodes.

The prototype is still being voiced; the current revision is a breadboard
build with a hand-cut acrylic plate.
